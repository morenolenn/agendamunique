Marienplatz Christkindlmarkt
20 Nov to 24 Dec
The city's main market, with the nativity market at St. Peter, music from the town hall balcony (Sunday to Thursday, 5:15pm) and the Himmelswerkstatt, a free workshop for kids aged 6 to 12. Monday to Saturday 10am to 9pm, Sunday until 8pm, 24 December until 2pm. Closed on 22 November.
